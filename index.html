Server Rules
1. Fair Play & Modifications
  • No Unfair Software: Hacked clients, auto-clickers, macros, and any custom scripts are strictly prohibited.
  • No Movement Modifications: Fly, speedhacks, elytra boosts, and similar movement mods are banned.
  • No Utility/Assistance Mods: Radars, X-Ray / ESP, health/hitbox indicators, Freecam, and auto-place mods are forbidden.
  • No Interface & Crafting Mods: Auto-sorting inventory tools, scroll-wheel tweaks, and custom crafting interface modifications are not allowed.
  • No Seed Searching: Finding, sharing, or attempting to recreate the world seed is strictly prohibited.
2. Economy & Alt Accounts
  • In-Game Trading Only: Any real-money trading (RMT / IRL Trading) is strictly forbidden.
  • No Cross-Server Trading: Exchanging items or currency from our server for goods on other projects is banned.
  • No External Gambling: Off-platform casinos, lotteries, and betting are prohibited.
  • No Boost & Invite Rewards: Offering in-game items/perks for Discord server boosts or inviting friends is not allowed.
  • Account Limit: You may use a maximum of 5 accounts per player (including alt accounts).
3. Bugs & Exploits
  • No Duplication: Item duping, as well as attempting or building dupe mechanisms, will result in an immediate ban.
  • No Exploiting Vulnerabilities: Abusing glitches, game bugs, or server errors is strictly forbidden.
  • Report All Glitches: You are required to immediately report any bugs, exploits, or rulebreakers to the staff team.
4. Community & Chat Standards
  • Respect Everyone: Harassment, bullying, doxxing (sharing private personal info), and wishing harm on others are banned.
  • No Discrimination: Hate speech, slurs, and toxic discrimination of any kind are strictly forbidden.
  • Keep Chat Clean: Spamming, flooding, encouraging spam, and abusing sound/mic-spamming in voice chat are prohibited.
  • No Unsolicited Advertising: Promotion of third-party projects or links is banned everywhere except in designated promo channels.
  • No Impersonation: Pretending to be a staff member or administrator is prohibited.
  • No Ban Evasion: Logging in on alternative accounts while banned will result in a permanent ban across all accounts.
The Golden Rule: Use common sense. Just because a specific harmful action isn't explicitly listed here doesn't mean it is allowed.
